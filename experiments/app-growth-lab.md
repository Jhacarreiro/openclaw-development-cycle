# App Growth Lab — B2C Mobile Acquisition Experiment

**Status:** proposed benchmark / candidate project; **not started**, not validated, and not a shipped feature of Development Cycle.

## Problem

Building consumer mobile apps with AI agents has become much easier. Acquiring real users sustainably remains difficult. We need a repeatable, evidence-driven way to understand and improve the acquisition funnel for iOS and Android B2C applications.

## Mission for a future, separately authorized Development Cycle run

Produce the smallest useful end-to-end solution for **one real B2C mobile app in one market**. The target is not a generic marketing dashboard: it is a practical process that connects a product's value proposition to acquisition hypotheses, lightweight experiments, measured outcomes and an informed next decision.

### Questions the solution must help answer

- Who is most likely to need this app, and what evidence supports that view?
- Where might those people be reachable?
- What positioning, messages or channels are worth testing, and why?
- What is the smallest credible test and its stopping criterion?
- How can results be measured without conflating visits, installs, activation and retention?
- What should change after observing outcomes?

### Minimum capabilities

1. Capture one app's positioning, audience, market, monetization assumptions and baseline evidence.
2. Explore contextually relevant acquisition approaches with short trade-offs. **Do not impose a fixed number or taxonomy of alternatives.** Zero viable options must be representable.
3. Define a bounded acquisition experiment: hypothesis, channels or actions, measurable outcome, budget/effort and stop conditions.
4. Ingest manually entered results or local CSV data without requiring paid APIs.
5. Compare observations with the original hypothesis and propose the next decision, with provenance and uncertainty.
6. Preserve a concise record of decisions, experiments, observations and limitations.

### Scope and safety

- One app and one geography, with an explicit timeboxed experiment.
- Prefer a small practical workflow. Software is optional where a simpler process solves the problem.
- No automatic advertising spend, third-party publication, scraping, contact outreach, account changes, paid services, or production deploys.
- No invented market research or fabricated results. Clearly label synthetic sample data.
- No analytics/integration platform, multi-tenant auth, or speculative features in the first run.
- Choose implementation, architecture and UX according to the problem; no prescribed framework or technology.
- Use ordinary behavior-based validation; avoid tautological or change-detector tests.

## Expected deliverable

A usable local solution showing one complete cycle from problem framing through acquisition experiment specification, observed/imported result and a justified decision about what to do next. Include usage instructions and a demonstration with clearly labeled representative data. **Building a tool alone does not prove acquisition works; only real-world outcomes can do that.**

## Why this is a Decision Multiverse candidate

An acquisition funnel contains consequential choices about customer segment, message, distribution and product value. This future run may provide evidence to reconstruct some of these choices after completion. The post-run decision map must not invent alternatives or reasoning, and speculative branches must not be presented as measured outcomes.
